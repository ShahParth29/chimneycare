About ChimneyCare

Our Story

Dedicated to clean, safe, and smoke-free kitchens.

ChimneyCare was founded with a single mission: to bring professional reliability, transparent parts pricing, and certified workmanship to kitchen ventilation across India.

Modern Indian cooking involves heavy spices and deep frying, requiring kitchen chimneys to operate at peak efficiency. Without regular maintenance, grease buildup causes motor failure, high noise, and fire hazards. We bridge this gap with scheduled Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), genuine OEM spare parts, and background-checked technicians.

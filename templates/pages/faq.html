Frequently Asked Questions

General & Cleaning Services

How often should I get my kitchen chimney deep cleaned?

For standard Indian cooking with daily frying and spices, we recommend deep cleaning every 2 to 3 months. If you cook lightly, a service every 4–6 months is sufficient. Our AMC plans are tailored around these exact usage cycles.

What is included in a One-Time Deep Cleaning?

Our deep cleaning includes complete disassembly of baffle/mesh filters, chemical degreasing, motor and blower fan blade cleansing, oil collector tray drainage and degreasing, exterior stainless steel / glass polishing, and duct airflow inspection.

How does the AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) work?

We offer 3-month (1 visit), 6-month (2 visits), and 12-month (4 visits) plans. All plans include 100% of labour charges, priority scheduling, and discounts on replacement spare parts. You will never pay unexpected labour fees during scheduled AMC visits.

Repairs, Spares & Technicians

Why are technician contact details hidden until confirmation?

To ensure optimal service quality, technician safety, and correct parts allocation, our operations desk conducts a brief telephonic confirmation within 24 hours of your request. Once confirmed and assigned by our team, full technician credentials become visible on your tracking portal.

Are the replacement spare parts genuine?

Yes. All parts (baffle filters, motors, PCB boards, carbon filters, and exhaust ducts) are sourced directly from authorized OEM channels or heavy-gauge certified manufacturers and carry a service guarantee.

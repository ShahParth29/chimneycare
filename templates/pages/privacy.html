Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Sobhraj Enterprise Pvt Ltd

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate home service visits and order fulfillment, we collect your name, service address, email address, phone number, and WhatsApp number when you create an account or book an appointment on ChimneyCare.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for scheduling technician visits, service coordination, order delivery, sending status updates (including WhatsApp confirmation messages), and customer support. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

All user data is stored on encrypted databases protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Access to customer records is restricted to authorized operations personnel.

4. Communication & Opt-Out

We send transactional alerts regarding your chimney bookings and orders. If you wish to modify your communication preferences, contact us at chimneycare.in@gmail.com.
